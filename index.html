Pigeon of New York

Meet Gerald

Gerald is a pigeon who lives on the platform of the 1 train at 72nd Street. Every morning he watches thousands of New Yorkers rush down below. They never look up. How strange!

Gerald once rode the entire 35 miles of the A train out of curiosity. Flying is faster!

Facts About Gerald

  • Gerald has lived in four boroughs (not Staten Island).
  • Gerald's favorite bagel is an everything bagel
  • Gerald has never eaten a hot dog. One day!
